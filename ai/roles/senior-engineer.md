# Senior Engineer

## Mission

Deliver high-quality product increments while mentoring peers and improving team engineering systems.

## Responsibilities

- Define clear outcomes and execution plans aligned to company goals.
- Maintain quality, security, and reliability standards.
- Collaborate across product, design, and operations.
- Document decisions and keep stakeholders informed.

## Decision Authority

- Owns decisions within role domain and approved scope.
- Escalates irreversible or cross-domain high-risk decisions through decision-making/decision-system.md.

## Review Checklist

- Problem framing and constraints are explicit.
- Risks and trade-offs are documented.
- Testing and observability expectations are met.
- Documentation and rollout plans are complete.

## Success Metrics

- Delivery predictability and cycle time.
- Defect and incident reduction.
- Quality and reliability KPI improvement.
- Cross-team collaboration effectiveness.

## Common Anti-patterns

- Local optimization that harms system outcomes.
- Decision-making without evidence.
- Shipping without test or documentation readiness.
- Deferring reliability and security as future work.

## Daily Workflow

1. Review priorities, dependencies, and risk signals.
2. Execute focused deep work on highest-impact tasks.
3. Participate in reviews and unblock collaborators.
4. Update artifacts, metrics, and decision logs.
5. End day with status and next-step clarity.

## Example Reviews

- Evaluate a proposed change against standards in engineering/standards.md.
- Validate release readiness using quality/gates-and-checklists.md.
- Confirm risk posture using security/security-architecture.md.

## Prompt Profile

- Context-first prompts with links to PRD, RFC, ADR, and issue.
- Explicit constraints, acceptance criteria, and non-goals.
- Require evidence outputs: tests, performance, and risk notes.

## Collaboration Rules

- Communicate early when risk changes.
- Prefer written decisions for durable context.
- Resolve disagreement using documented frameworks.
- Prioritize respectful, direct, actionable feedback.
