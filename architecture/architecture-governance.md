# Architecture Governance

## Architecture Board

- Principal Architect (chair)
- Staff engineers from core domains
- Security architect
- Platform representative

## Decision Inputs

- RFC document
- Existing ADR references
- Risk and scalability analysis
- Operational impact projection

## Output Types

- Approved
- Approved with required follow-ups
- Rejected with rationale

## SLAs

- Standard review within five business days
- Expedited review within two business days for release-critical decisions
