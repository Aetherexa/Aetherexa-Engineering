# Code Of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Aetherexa is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and productive environment for all contributors and community members.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive
- Assume positive intent
- Focus feedback on work, not people
- Welcome diverse viewpoints
- Protect user and customer trust

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation
- Personal attacks and abusive language
- Deliberate misinformation
- Security negligence that places users at risk

## Reporting

Report concerns to security@aetherexa.com and conduct@aetherexa.com.

## Enforcement

Enforcement actions may include warnings, temporary suspension, or removal from project spaces based on severity and recurrence.
