# Incident Management

## Severity Model

- Sev0: existential availability, security, or data risk
- Sev1: major user-impacting outage
- Sev2: partial degradation
- Sev3: minor impact

## Lifecycle

1. Detect and triage
2. Assign incident commander
3. Mitigate and communicate
4. Recover and validate
5. Complete postmortem with tracked actions
