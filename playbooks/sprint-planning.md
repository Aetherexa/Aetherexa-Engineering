# Sprint Planning

## Inputs

- Prioritized backlog
- Capacity forecast
- Risk and dependency map
- Production health signals

## Process

1. Reconfirm sprint objective.
2. Select outcome-oriented stories.
3. Attach acceptance criteria and test strategy.
4. Identify blockers and owners.
5. Lock sprint commitment and publish plan.

## Outputs

- Sprint goal
- Committed scope with owners
- Risk register updates
- Definition-of-done checkpoints
