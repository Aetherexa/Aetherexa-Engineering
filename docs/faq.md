# FAQ

## Why an Engineering OS?

To standardize high-quality execution across all products while preserving team autonomy.

## When is an RFC required?

For non-trivial, cross-team, risky, or architecture-impacting changes.

## How do we handle disagreements?

Use the decision framework in decision-making/decision-system.md and escalate via defined paths.

## What if standards conflict with delivery urgency?

Document risk acceptance, obtain appropriate approval, and create follow-up remediation tasks.
