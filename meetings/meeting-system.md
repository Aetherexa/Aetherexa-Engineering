# Meeting System

Each meeting uses a standard format: purpose, attendees, agenda, expected outputs, follow-up actions.

## Engineering Kickoff

- Purpose: align on scope, constraints, and readiness
- Attendees: engineering, product, design, QA
- Agenda: objectives, architecture, timeline, risks
- Outputs: kickoff notes, owners, risk register
- Follow-up: publish execution plan

## Sprint Planning

- Purpose: commit sprint outcomes
- Attendees: delivery team, engineering manager, product manager
- Agenda: goals, capacity, story commitments
- Outputs: sprint goal and committed backlog
- Follow-up: dependency confirmation

## Architecture Review

- Purpose: validate architecture decisions
- Attendees: architects, staff engineers, owning team
- Agenda: problem, options, trade-offs, rollout
- Outputs: approval decision, ADR actions
- Follow-up: decision log update

## RFC Review

- Purpose: evaluate significant proposal readiness
- Attendees: cross-functional reviewers
- Agenda: proposal summary, open questions, risks
- Outputs: approve, request changes, or reject
- Follow-up: track action items

## ADR Review

- Purpose: validate decision records and long-term implications
- Attendees: architects and domain owners
- Agenda: context, decision, consequences
- Outputs: ADR approval and publication
- Follow-up: architecture index update

## Weekly Engineering Sync

- Purpose: cross-team alignment
- Attendees: engineering leadership and team representatives
- Agenda: progress, blockers, incidents, dependencies
- Outputs: decision list and escalations
- Follow-up: owner-driven action closure

## Product Review

- Purpose: validate user and business outcomes
- Attendees: product, engineering, design, GTM stakeholders
- Agenda: metrics, demos, roadmap decisions
- Outputs: priority adjustments
- Follow-up: backlog updates

## Design Critique

- Purpose: improve UX and implementation clarity
- Attendees: design plus feature team
- Agenda: user flows, accessibility, implementation impacts
- Outputs: approved design directions
- Follow-up: design spec updates

## Incident Review

- Purpose: ensure mitigation quality and learning
- Attendees: incident responders and stakeholders
- Agenda: timeline, root cause, corrective actions
- Outputs: postmortem and owners
- Follow-up: track preventive actions

## Retrospective

- Purpose: improve team system
- Attendees: sprint team
- Agenda: wins, pain points, experiments
- Outputs: top improvement actions
- Follow-up: verify next sprint adoption

## One-on-One

- Purpose: growth, alignment, and support
- Attendees: manager and report
- Agenda: goals, blockers, feedback, development
- Outputs: agreed actions
- Follow-up: progress check in next session

## Quarterly Planning

- Purpose: set strategic outcomes and investment balance
- Attendees: leadership and team leads
- Agenda: priorities, capacity, risk, hiring, platform investments
- Outputs: quarter plan and dependency map
- Follow-up: publish execution scorecard

## Release Readiness Review

- Purpose: decide go or no-go
- Attendees: release manager, engineering, QA, product, security
- Agenda: quality gates, incident risk, rollback preparedness
- Outputs: release decision
- Follow-up: release communications and monitoring plan
