# Onboarding Playbook

## First Week

- Environment setup and toolchain validation
- Read company handbook and engineering principles
- Complete security and privacy training
- Ship first small change with mentor support

## First 30 Days

- Complete learning path for role and domain
- Participate in design and review cycles
- Own one scoped feature or subsystem task

## First 90 Days

- Lead one end-to-end delivery stream
- Contribute an RFC or ADR
- Mentor new contributors on standards
